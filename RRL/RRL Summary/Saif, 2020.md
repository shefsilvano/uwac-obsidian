# Essence 
* Use UAV as a the backhaul for the ground user devices (victims and first responders) to extend the coverage area of the LTE **services**
* UAV as a repeater for Ground User's cellular access request 
> [!PDF|yellow] [[Unmanned_Aerial_Vehicles_for_Post-Disaster_Communication_Networks.pdf#page=1&selection=256,0,277,2&color=yellow|Unmanned_Aerial_Vehicles_for_Post-Disaster_Communication_Networks, p.1]]
> **Device-to-device (D2D) communication** has been proposed to enable 5G technology to increase system capacity and energy efficiency under the controlled cellular system 
 * ([[Unmanned_Aerial_Vehicles_for_Post-Disaster_Communication_Networks.pdf#page=1&selection=312,0,346,8&color=yellow| Use of 5G]])
> In this context, the multi-hop D2D communications are employed to **extend the coverage to user devices that are located out of the coverage of the cellular system.**
